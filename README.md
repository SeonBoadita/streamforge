# StreamForge — Features

StreamForge is a creator-focused streaming and video platform where users can **watch live streams, discover videos and shorts, interact with creators, and support them**. It combines live content, recorded content, short-form content, and creator communities in one platform.

### 🔴 Live Streaming
Users can discover and watch creators who are currently live. Each live stream includes a video player, viewer count, stream information, creator details, and real-time interaction through live chat and reactions. Users can follow or support creators while watching.

### ⚡ Shorts
Users can discover short-form vertical videos designed for quick and continuous viewing. Shorts can be liked, commented on, shared, and connected to the creator's profile and other content.

### 🎬 Videos
Creators can publish longer recorded videos for users to watch at any time. Users can browse videos by category, search for specific content, like, comment, share, save content, and discover related videos.

### ✂️ Clips
Users can create short clips from interesting moments in live streams. Clips can be shared independently while still linking back to the original stream and creator.

### 👤 Creator Profiles
Every creator has a dedicated profile containing their information, followers, live streams, videos, shorts, clips, schedule, and other community content. Users can follow creators and keep up with their latest activity.

### 🔎 Discovery & Search
The platform helps users discover new content through the home page, trending content, categories, recommendations, popular creators, live streams, videos, shorts, and clips. Users can also search directly for creators and content.

### 💬 Community & Interaction
Users can interact with creators and other viewers through live chat, comments, likes, reactions, sharing, and follows. Live streams also support community-focused features such as moderation and subscriber-only interactions.

### 📅 Stream Scheduling
Creators can schedule upcoming live streams with a title, description, category, and starting time. Users can view upcoming streams and set reminders so they don't miss them.

### 🔔 Notifications
Users receive updates about creators and content they care about, such as creators going live, upcoming streams, new videos or shorts, new followers, subscriptions, mentions, and other important activity.

### ❤️ Following & Subscriptions
Users can follow their favourite creators and see their latest activity in one place. Creators can also offer subscription or supporter options for users who want to provide additional support.

### 💎 Creator Support
Users can support creators through subscriptions, memberships, and tips. Creators can provide additional benefits or exclusive experiences to their supporters.

### 📊 Creator Dashboard
Creators have a dedicated dashboard to manage their streams and content while viewing important information such as viewers, followers, views, watch time, engagement, and earnings.

### 🛡️ Moderation & Safety
Creators and moderators can manage their communities by deleting messages, timing out or banning users, enabling slow mode, restricting chat, blocking unwanted words, and handling reports.

### 🌐 Web3 Features
StreamForge is designed to support optional Web3 features where they provide real value. Users can eventually connect wallets, send crypto tips to creators, earn supporter badges, and access exclusive events through NFT ownership.

### 📱 Complete User Experience
The platform is designed around the complete creator journey:

**Discover → Watch → Interact → Follow → Subscribe/Support → Create & Share → Build a Community**

This allows StreamForge to function not just as a streaming website, but as a **complete platform for creators and their communities**.
